Sky Chicken – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026  ·  Developer: Rohit Tiwari  ·  Contact: tiwari.rohit761@gmail.com

The short version

Sky Chicken has no accounts and we (the developer) do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is handled by Google services that the game uses, described below.

Data stored on your device

Coins, levels, unlocked items, settings, best scores and daily progress are stored locally on your phone. If Android's backup feature is on, Android may include this data in your device backup in your own Google account. Uninstalling the game deletes the local data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show one optional rewarded video ad when you choose to watch it to continue a run. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use device identifiers such as your advertising ID, your IP address and information about how the ad performed, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in the game's consent options if shown, or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Google Play Games (leaderboard)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your best distance is submitted to the leaderboard and Google processes your player profile according to Google's privacy policy. Signing in is optional; the game works without it.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional coin packs are bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment details. Coins are virtual items with no cash value.

Install referrer

When you install the game from a friend's challenge link, the game reads the Google Play install referrer once to open that challenge. This contains only the challenge code, not personal information.

Sharing

When you share a score card or challenge, the image and text you choose go to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. Challenge codes contain a game course number and a score only.

Children

Sky Chicken is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and data deletion

You can uninstall the game to remove its local data, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage Google Play Games data in your Google account. For any question or request, contact us at the email above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
